Indian Government

Understand the basic structure of government and learn how different institutions work.

🇮🇳 What is Government?

Government is the system through which a country is administered and public functions are carried out. In India, government functions are carried out at different levels.

🇮🇳

Union Government

Works at the national level and deals with matters assigned to the Union.

🏢

State Government

Works at the state level and handles matters assigned to states.

🏘️

Local Government

Works at the local level through institutions such as municipalities and panchayats.

🏛️ Parliament of India

Parliament is the Union legislature of India. It consists of the President and two Houses.

🏛️

Lok Sabha

The House of the People. Its members are directly elected by voters.

🏛️

Rajya Sabha

The Council of States. It represents the states and Union Territories in Parliament.

📊 Three Levels of Government

2

3

Local Level
